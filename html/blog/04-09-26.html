Rant about iOS 27 my beloved

Friday, 4 September 2026

The iOS 27 betas have been so smooth for me. I genuinely never thought we’d see a good OS update after the whole iOS 26 failure but this next update fixes and adds so many QoL things such as the liquid glass slider, a genuinely great new Siri and much better performance overall. People were so pissed off when 26 came out with that ugly forced liquid glass design and honestly I felt like that for a while too, but now it’s so much more refined and customizable and Siri is merged insanely well with the dynamic island.

Now regarding Siri we in Europe are basically not allowed to have fun but you can get around that restriction by using a non EU App Store account anyway. I'm also not gonna repeat the not so unpopular take that Apple is late to the AI race because obviously they are, but this new Siri is integrated really well into the phone. You can basically search through anything in your messages, emails or files and that’s genuinely one of the biggest QoL changes iPhones were missing. Yeah I know it’s mostly locked to Apple apps right now but hear me out am I crazy for thinking they’ll probably open this up to other apps eventually..? Gmail and messaging apps for example that would be great. I have no idea how they’d actually pull that off when it comes to indexing and caching all that information though so maybe opening it up isn’t as easy as it sounds.

Speaking of that this update takes up so much storage mostly because of all the AI stuff and that’s depressing. After all these years you’d think Apple would have figured storage management out but apparently not. How hard can it be to make a better caching system? Or crazy idea, just let users manually clear an app cache?

Now performance wise it’s actually kind of flawless. Of course there were some small visual bugs here and there in the first few betas, but now that we’ve reached beta 8 this week I’m seeing close to zero visual bugs. No overheating either on a 15 Pro mind you! And the keyboard finally seems to be fixed so that’s nice.
Apple said this update would focus heavily on performance, and for once they actually delivered on that. I think I read that the final release build should be coming sometime this month.

Anyway: good software for once.
Would recommend updating.
Unless you’re still using a 13 mini.
In that case good luck.
